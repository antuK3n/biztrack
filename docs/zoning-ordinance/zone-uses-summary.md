# Malabon City Ordinance No. 24-2018 — Article V (Zone Regulations) digest

Source: Article V, Sections 1-5 (pp. 26-53 of the ordinance). Section 2 base zones below; Sections 3-5 follow at the end.

## Section 2 — Regulations in Base Zones

### Section 2.1 RESIDENTIAL 1 - ZONE (R1-Z)

**Allowed uses (27):**

- Single-detached dwelling units
- Semi-detached family dwelling units, e.g. duplex
- Townhouses
- Residential Subdivisions approved per P.D. 957 standards
- Home occupation for the practice of one's profession such as offices of physicians, surgeons, dentists, architects, engineers, lawyers, and other professionals or for engaging home business such as dressmaking, tailoring, baking, running a sari-sari store/neighbourhood convenience store and the like, provided that: the number of persons engaged in such business/industry shall not exceed five (5), inclusive of owner; there shall be no change in the outside appearance of the building premises; in no case shall more than 20% of the building be used for said home occupation; no home occupation shall be conducted in any customary accessory uses cited above; no traffic shall be generated in greater volume than would normally be expected in a residential neighborhood and any parking need shall be met off the street and in a place other than the required front yard; and no equipment or process shall be used which creates noise, vibration, glare, fumes, odors and electrical interference detectable to the normal senses and visual or audible interference in any radio or television receiver or causes fluctuations in line voltage off the premises
- Home Industry classified as cottage industry, provided that: such home industry shall not occupy more than thirty percent (30%) of the floor area of the dwelling unit; there shall be no change or alteration in the outside appearance of the dwelling unit and shall not be a hazard or nuisance; it shall be classified as non-pollutive/non-hazardous as provided in this integrated ZO; allotted capitalization shall not exceed the capitalization as set by the DTI; and such shall consider the provisions pertaining to customary accessory uses, traffic and equipment/process under Home Occupation of this section
- Recreational facilities for the exclusive use of the members of the family residing within the premises: Swimming pool
- Recreational facilities for the exclusive use of the members of the family residing within the premises: Tennis courts
- Recreational facilities for the exclusive use of the members of the family residing within the premises: Basketball courts
- Parks and Open Spaces
- Nursery/Elementary School
- Tutorial services
- Clubhouse and other social centers
- Sports club
- Religious use as churches or similar place of worship
- Multi-purpose/Barangay hall
- Clinic, nursing and convalescing home, health center, Day-care centers
- Plant nursery
- Customary accessory uses incidental to any of the principal uses, provided that such accessory uses shall not include any activity conducted for monetary gain or commercial purposes: Servants quarters
- Customary accessory uses incidental to any of the principal uses, provided that such accessory uses shall not include any activity conducted for monetary gain or commercial purposes: Private garage
- Customary accessory uses incidental to any of the principal uses, provided that such accessory uses shall not include any activity conducted for monetary gain or commercial purposes: Guardhouse
- Customary accessory uses incidental to any of the principal uses, provided that such accessory uses shall not include any activity conducted for monetary gain or commercial purposes: Laundries
- Customary accessory uses incidental to any of the principal uses, provided that such accessory uses shall not include any activity conducted for monetary gain or commercial purposes: Non-commercial garages
- Customary accessory uses incidental to any of the principal uses, provided that such accessory uses shall not include any activity conducted for monetary gain or commercial purposes: Houses for pets such as dogs, birds, rabbits and the like of not more than 4.00 sq. m. in floor area
- Customary accessory uses incidental to any of the principal uses, provided that such accessory uses shall not include any activity conducted for monetary gain or commercial purposes: Pump houses
- Customary accessory uses incidental to any of the principal uses, provided that such accessory uses shall not include any activity conducted for monetary gain or commercial purposes: Generator houses
- Customary accessory uses incidental to any of the principal uses: Other small power plant and machineries customarily used in connection with churches, schools, museum, parks, playground, community centers, golf course and dwelling and their accessory buildings, provided that such power plant and other machinery are so placed as to cause the least inconvenience to adjoining residences, and provided that they do not cause serious injury or nuisance to occupants through fumes or gases, dust, smoke, noise and vibrations or other similar nuisance, and provided that such power plant, if other than electric, should be used only in cases of emergency and power failure, provided further that if the power employed is electric motor and used exclusively for air conditioning, for elevators, for water pumps and for household appliances such as washing machine, refrigerators and the like, any number of horse-power necessary is allowed

**Notes:** Low density residential use. Per the National Building Code, R-1 Zone is characterized mainly by low-rise single-detached and duplex residential buildings for exclusive use as single (nuclear) family dwellings. Building Regulations: in accordance with the relevant provisions of the NBC, PD 957 and this Ordinance; areas located in flood risk shall observe safety regulations under Flood Overlay Zone.

## Section 3 — Regulations in Special Use Areas

Areas within the City that are all existing projects allowed by virtue of economic and historical value, and other cases that cannot be classified at the time of enactment of this Ordinance due to lack of guidelines. **A special use permit is required for each of the following uses**, subject to the terms and conditions prescribed. These are not a flat list of permitted uses — each entry is a set of siting/operating conditions layered on top of whatever the base zone allows.

**A. Cemeteries** — per P.D. 856 (Sanitation Code): at least 20 m from any dwelling unit (and no house within the same distance of a burial ground); 50 m from either side of a river or from any source of water supply; not in areas with high water table, water recharge aquifers, water bearing rocks or good soil permeability; preferably in sparsely populated areas but not outside the city, or on the periphery of the town proper and away from the city water system; preferably on ground free from flood hazards; not obstructing the opening of future streets and arterial grounds; the number of burials within the city must be considered (deaths vs. burial permits); all HLURB memorial-park rules and P.D. 856; Sections 102 and 203 of the National Building Code (P.D. 1096); all sanitation and water code requirements.

**B. Funeral establishment** — all IRR governing the processing of funeral establishments under E.O. 648 of HLURB, Article IV Section 5 (c). Requirements: vicinity map (1:10,000) showing the exact site and adjacent land uses within a 500 m radius; site development plan (1:200) showing boundaries, building layout, future expansion, entrance/exit and parking; TCT or contract of sale/lease with approved survey plan if available; sanitary clearance from the City Health Office under P.D. 856; floor plan; ECC from DENR prior to building permit application.

**C. Filling stations** — must conform to standards set by the Energy Regulatory Board; at least 200 m from the nearest schools, churches, hospitals and similar institutions; shall not constitute safety hazards in a community developed entirely for residential purposes; buffer strip and adequate firefighting equipment required. (Note: C-1 Section 2.7 states filling stations must conform to Department of Energy standards and imposes a 1 km separation from existing gasoline/LPG filling stations — the two provisions differ.)

**D. Open storage** — at least 200 m from schools, churches, hospitals and similar institutions; sound maintenance is the sole responsibility of the proponent.

**E. Abattoir/slaughterhouse** — guided by HLURB Board Resolution R-650 s. 1999; at least 200 m from residential areas, schools, churches and other places of assembly, courts or public offices; accessible to transportation; at least 25 m from markets and other food establishments; located within reasonable distance from residential and commercial areas and not within the same premises as public markets; proper waste disposal, odor control and abatement; ECC required; IRR of Chapter IV (Markets and Slaughterhouse/Abattoir) of P.D. 856; the proponent must obtain sworn statements from owners of immediately adjacent land as a prerequisite to the special use permit; construction and maintenance to conform to DOH and National Meat Inspection Commission standards; HLURB locational guidelines and design standards; other concerned agencies.

**F. Cockpits** — shall be located in parks and recreational zones, and at least 200 m from the nearest residence or institutional use; adequate parking and loading/unloading areas sized for the maximum expected spectators during major derbies; applicant must maintain the premises in sanitary condition; other pertinent national and local laws apply.

**G. Base stations** — base stations of cellular mobile telephone service, paging services, trucking service, wireless local loop and other wireless communication services. HLURB locational guidelines under Resolution No. 626, series of 998 [sic]; the sound of the station is the exclusive responsibility of the proponent/operator; where public welfare demands, base and transmitting stations **may be located in some residential zones**, provided the preceding conditions are strictly complied with and no interference with or damage to local electrical appliances or other property is likely; guidelines of the National Telecommunication Commission and the Department of Transportation and Communication.

**H. Materials Recovery Facility (MRF) / transfer station** — site must be recommended by the City Environmental and Natural Resources Office (CENRO); adequate fencing to prevent scattering of wastes; general maintenance is the sole responsibility of the proponent; RA 9003 (Ecological Solid Waste Management Act of 2000) and City/DOH sanitary requirements; other conditions and safeguards to be recommended by the Local Zoning Committee.

**I. Billboard / regulated signs** — allowed only along lots fronting the National Road. Design regulations include: locational, structural and DPWH billboard clearances; setbacks keyed to road right-of-way width (ROW 30.00 m & above → 8.00 m front / 5.00 m side / 5.00 m rear; 25.00–29.00 → 6.00/3.00/3.00; 20.00–24.00 → 5.00/3.00/3.00; 10.00–19.00 → 5.00/2.00/2.00; below 10.00 → 5.00/2.00/2.00); minimum 100 m radius between billboards, with earlier-permitted signs enjoying preference; no obstruction of traffic signs, landscape, fire escapes/exits/standpipes or openings; incombustible materials in highly restrictive fire zones; display surfaces of metal, glass or approved plastics; **roof signs are not allowed**; non-rigid banner/tarpaulin structures kept clear of power lines per NBC IRR Rule XIII.1; clearances from high voltage power lines per the Philippine Electrical Code; regulated, temporary and LED signs over or along public thoroughfares, center islands and road rights-of-way are strictly prohibited (MMDA Regulation No. 96-009); no signs within sidewalks, flyovers, interchanges, traffic signages, communications posts, LRT/MRT structures, ROWs, posts or waiting sheds; historical-site and tourist-destination markers with sponsor advertising only in the periphery and under a MOU/MOA witnessed by the LGU and MMDA; no signs on trees, electric/lighting posts, center islands, side strips or fences defacing natural landscape or seascape; no signs crossing or straddling carriageways; signs in covered areas must be taken down or turned off on PAGASA announcement of a low pressure area or weather disturbance, at the owner's expense if MMDA must do it. LGUs in Metro Manila may impose stricter regulations. Refer to MMDA Memorandum Circular No. 10 s. 2011 and NBC Rule XX – Signs of the revised IRR of PD 1096.

**J. Transport terminals** — terminals for passenger buses and other transport vehicles accommodating more than three (3) units, and terminals for passenger jeepneys and taxis accommodating more than six (6) units at any given time, shall be located at a reasonable distance from residential zones. Required facilities within the terminal compound: boarding/loading space (≥ 1.5 × the actual dimension of a vehicle unit × the estimated maximum number of units boarding at any time); temporary parking space for queuing units (actual vehicle dimension × estimated maximum units on the premises at any time); disembarking/arrival area (≥ 1.5 × vehicle dimension × two); backing-up and maneuvering only within the compound; measures to confine noise, vibration and odors (structural enclosures, buffer yards); proper waste disposal with no direct discharge of waste oils and grease to public sewer/drainage, the operator being solely responsible for sanitary condition; no terminal-related activities outside the premises; a site development plan/layout at 1:500 must be submitted to the Zoning Official as part of the locational clearance application.

## Section 4 — Regulations in Overlay Zones

An overlay zone is a "transparent zone" overlain on a Base Zone or another Overlay Zone that provides an additional set (layer) of regulations, which may pertain to additionally allowable uses, building density and bulk, and building/structure design.

### 4.1 Flood Overlay Zone (FLD-OZ)
Areas identified as prone to flooding. Objective: protect lives and property from the harmful effects of flood.

- **Allowable uses:** "Allowable uses shall be as provided in the respective Base Zone, subject to the following additional regulations:" — the overlay **adds no uses and removes none**; it only adds construction requirements.
- **Adds (building density and bulk):** buildings must be flood-proofed by raising the ground floor above base flood elevation per the susceptibility table — Low (flood elevation 0–0.50 m) → ground floor above 0.50 m; Moderate (0.50–1.00 m) → above 1.0 m; High (1.00–2.0 m) → above 2.00 m; Very High (above 2.0 m) → above 3.00 m (base flood elevations from the CLUP 2018-2027 assessment unless DRRMO releases an update). On-stilt houses may be adopted; roof decks usable for evacuation; natural drainage system shall not be altered; utility connections (electricity, potable water, sewage) above the flood protection elevation; sustainable urban drainage systems (rainwater storage, green roofs) to reduce and reuse storm water run-off.
- Nearly every base zone in Section 2 cross-references this overlay ("areas located in flood risk shall observe safety regulations under the Flood Overlay Zone").

### 4.2 Ecotourism Overlay Zone (ETM-OZ)
Covers the area of all existing fishponds of Barangay Dampalit. Objective: attain the dual goals of environmental conservation and tourism economic development.

- **Adds uses** (in addition to those allowed in the Base Zone): dining facilities; water-oriented recreation/sports rental equipment shops; tourism oriented retail stores (e.g. souvenirs, foreign exchange shops, clothes, etc.).
- **Restricts:** usable area capped at 30% maximum of lot area for business operation, of which 10% of that 30% may be converted to land-based use for parking, sanitary facilities and the like. Dining and eco-tourism facilities must be one (1) storey; designs must conform to applicable Department of Tourism standards; buildings on stilts encouraged; electrical appliances raised at least 1000 mm above the finish floor line; impermeable paving outside building envelopes not allowed; firewalls along property lines not allowed; roadside friction to be minimized.

### 4.3 Heritage Overlay Zone (HTG-OZ)
Applied in areas with houses of ancestry. Objectives: preserve existing historic structures, and harmonize the design and construction of new ones with those historic structures.

- **Restricts uses:** for declared houses of ancestry, allowable uses are **limited to** single-detached residential; museum; and shops, offices, restaurants, craftsmen's workshops and retail outlets (only at the ground floor). For new construction, allowable uses are as provided in the base R-1 Zone.
- **Restricts bulk** (superseding the base R-1 zone): for declared houses of ancestry, the height and floor area of the existing building shall not be altered; for new construction, the building height limit within the buffer zone shall not exceed the roof apex of the declared heritage structure (NHCP 2012 guidelines).
- **Design regulations:** for declared houses of ancestry, repair and renovation (building and landscape) must maintain the original architectural design inside and out, and the size and appearance of business and other signs must blend with the period design of the house. For new construction, designs (building and landscape) must be similar to the period designs of the declared houses of ancestry.
- **Other:** gathering of natural and historical artifacts is subject to RA 10066.

## Section 5 — Zoning Incentives

- Density bonuses, such as through allowable building height increases, may be provided as incentives for projects that use CCA/DRRM technology or innovations — i.e. solar panels, rainwater harvesting, smart urban drainage systems, green architecture/building systems.
- Similar incentives may also be given to projects that provide wider setbacks, increased ground level open spaces, provide public infrastructure, or conserve heritage sites.
- No numeric bonus schedule, cap or application procedure is stated in Article V; both provisions are permissive ("may be provided").
