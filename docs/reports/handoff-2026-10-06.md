## Claim before work (`bd306d8`) — the big one

**Reverted by Ken on 6 October 2026** (`5db53073` on main): offices act on a case as before, under the 30 September shared-inbox rules.

The request named "Other Admin Offices", so **BPLO is unchanged**. For CHO, BFP,
CPDO, OBO and CENRO:

- An unheld case cannot be **opened** (`GET /assignments/{id}` → 403
  `Assign this application to yourself first.`) or **worked**. The queue card
  does not link until claimed.
- Once held, only the holder acts; colleagues read it (`can_act` false).
- **One rule for every door:** `App\Support\CaseHolder` (`mayAct`, `mayOpen`,
  `refusal`, `authorizeOn`, `claimRequiredFor`). Wired into approve, return,
  checks and classification (`AssignmentController::authorizeHolder`), the
  `show` endpoint, business-field edits, office-sheet corrections, officer
  requests (store/close), inspections (schedule, conduct, reschedule,
  reinspect, name inspector, `can_name_inspector`), and messages.
- **Messages:** an officer sees an application's conversation only while they
  hold that office's case, and then sees **all** of it. A previous holder loses
  it. Unheld cases' conversations are visible to nobody in the office. General
  enquiries and the admin line stay office-wide. BPLO keeps the 30 September
  per-officer "stretch" rule (`handled_by_user_id`).
- **Tests:** about 150 fixtures acted on unclaimed cases. They now claim first
  via `claimAs()` / `scopedFilingHeldByCho()` in `tests/Pest.php`. Tests that
  encoded the old rule were rewritten to the new rule and kept for BPLO.
  `ClaimBeforeWorkTest` covers the new rule. No assertion was loosened.

  the results had to be thrown away.
