# 1. Fixed and verified

## The two criticals

**Filings could never issue permits.** Approving one office's review sent a filing to
`for_inspection`, and the review sheet then keyed on the *filing's* status alone — so the five
offices that still owed a review lost their Approve button entirely. No approval, no inspection, no
permits, forever. `BIZ-2026-00958` was in that state.

Now the sheet keys on **this office's own assignment**. An office that still owes a review gets its
sheet; an office that has finished gets only the compact box. Both on one filing at once — which is
what per-office inspections created and what nothing in the screen modelled.

**Terminal filings could be revived.** Any office's approval dragged a `returned`, `rejected` or
`cancelled` filing back into `for_inspection` — proven on a copy of the register: `rejected →
for_inspection`, history row written, inspection booked. **101 rejected filings had that exposure.**
There is now a transition legality table with four guards at four depths.

## Found while fixing, reported by nobody

**Duplicate permits.** `approveAndIssue` minted permits *before* the status changed, and the status
change was a no-op when already approved — so a second inspection result silently issued **a second
numbered permit for every type**. The city would have had two Mayor's Permits for one business, both
verifiable. Proven: 4 permits where there should be 2.

that touches your live data will be shown to you before it runs.
